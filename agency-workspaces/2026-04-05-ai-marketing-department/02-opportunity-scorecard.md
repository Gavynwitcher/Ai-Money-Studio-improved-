# Opportunity Scorecard

## Summary

- Client: AI Marketing Department
- Goal: Land the first paying client in 30 days
- Decision: Start as a productized agency for founder-led service businesses that need offer clarity and a launch-ready acquisition system

## Opportunity Table

| Opportunity | Demand (1-5) | Competition (1-5) | Monetization (1-5) | Speed (1-5) | Complexity (1-5) | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| AI launch systems for founder-led service businesses | 4 | 3 | 4 | 5 | 2 | Pain is urgent, buyer is reachable, and scope can be productized into a 7-14 day engagement |
| Fractional AI content ops for B2B SaaS teams | 3 | 4 | 4 | 2 | 4 | Budgets can be larger but sales cycles are slower and proof expectations are higher |
| Local service lead-gen automation for home services | 4 | 4 | 4 | 3 | 3 | Strong demand, but crowded and often performance-priced with higher fulfillment pressure |

## Chosen Direction

- Primary opportunity: AI launch systems for founder-led service businesses with 5 to 50 employees
- Why it wins: This segment has high urgency, weak internal marketing systems, and short decision cycles. The agency can sell a clear transformation: move from vague offer plus inconsistent outreach to a defined offer, landing page, email sequence, and measurable lead-generation plan in 14 days.
- Risks: Limited brand proof, crowded AI positioning, and the temptation to widen scope too early

## Evidence

- QuickBooks reported on June 25, 2025 that 68% of surveyed US small businesses now use AI regularly and 74% say AI is boosting productivity, which supports demand for AI-enabled operating systems rather than education-only offers.
- Intuit and ICIC reported in their 2025 AI research that 89% of small businesses are currently leveraging AI and more than 60% reported increased productivity, indicating widespread adoption but also a capability gap.
- HubSpot reported in its 2025 AI trends coverage that 65% of marketing leaders planned to increase AI and automation investment during 2025, reinforcing demand for implementation support.
- Content Marketing Institute's 2025 B2B research found 45% of marketers lack a scalable content creation model and 47% struggle to measure content results, which maps directly to the agency's offer.
- Semrush's 2025 AI Overviews study indicates discussion/forum visibility and video carousels are becoming more important in search, which favors a lean strategy built around founder content plus search-aware pages.
