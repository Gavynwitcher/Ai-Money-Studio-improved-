# Offer And Positioning

## Offer Design

- Core problem: Founder-led service businesses know they need marketing, but their offer, message, content, and funnel are fragmented, which causes inconsistent lead flow and slow sales
- Transformation promised: Turn a vague service business into a clearly positioned offer with launch-ready assets and a measurable lead-generation system in 7 to 14 days
- Mechanism: Structured market validation, positioning sprint, search-aware content planning, launch asset production, and simple funnel instrumentation executed through the `$ai-marketing-department` workflow
- Price point: Opportunity Sprint at $1,500, Launch System Build at $4,000, Growth Optimization Retainer at $2,500 per month
- Packaging: Fixed-scope productized services with optional monthly optimization after the build

## Persona Snapshot

- Buyer: Founder, operator, or owner of a service business with a small team and no senior in-house marketer
- Trigger event: Lead flow is inconsistent, a launch is stalling, or they are using AI tools without a coherent acquisition system
- Main objection: "We can piece this together ourselves with freelancers and ChatGPT."
- Buying motivation: Faster clarity, faster execution, and a path to booked calls without hiring a full team

## Positioning

- USP: We turn founder-led service businesses into launch-ready growth systems in 14 days by combining AI-assisted strategy, content, and funnel execution in one fixed-scope engagement.
- Positioning statement: AI Marketing Department is a productized growth agency for founder-led service businesses that need a validated offer, sharper positioning, and a simple acquisition system without hiring a full marketing team.
- Emotional angle: Stop guessing and finally know what to sell, how to say it, and how to turn attention into calls.
- Logical angle: One engagement replaces weeks of fragmented strategy, copywriting, content planning, and funnel setup.
- Social proof angle: Built on a codified seven-phase operating system instead of ad hoc freelancer output.

## Voice Guide

- Sound like: Direct, commercially literate, practical, founder-aware, and allergic to fluff
- Do not sound like: Trend-chasing, hype-heavy, jargon-soaked, or vague about numbers and deliverables

## Homepage Message

- Headline: Build a Client-Getting Marketing System in 14 Days
- Subheadline: We help founder-led service businesses clarify the offer, sharpen the message, and launch the content and funnel assets needed to generate measurable demand.
- Primary CTA: Book an Opportunity Sprint
