# Client Brief

- Client: AI Marketing Department
- Date opened: 2026-04-05
- Engagement: Launch System Build

## Business Snapshot

- Offer type: Productized service
- Market: Marketing systems for founders and SMBs
- ICP: Founders and small business owners who need a validated offer and acquisition system
- Primary goal: Land the first paying client in 30 days
- Current stage: Pre-launch, with the service model and operating templates in place but no live acquisition engine
- Current channels: None active yet; initial focus will be LinkedIn, founder-led short-form video, search-oriented website content, and direct outreach
- Existing assets: `$ai-marketing-department` skill, client workspace scaffolds, agency operating manual, Codex-run workflow, and a local delivery system
- Constraints: Solo operator, limited proof and case studies, no established audience, low initial paid budget, and need for fast path to first revenue

## Working Assumptions

- The fastest path to first revenue is a narrowly defined productized service for founder-led service businesses rather than a broad "full-service marketing" pitch.
- The initial wedge should emphasize offer clarity, launch assets, and lightweight funnel setup instead of long-horizon SEO retainers.
- The first clients are more likely to come from direct founder-led distribution and outbound than from waiting for inbound search.
- Pricing should be low-friction enough to close an early client while remaining high enough to signal strategic value.

## Success Metrics

- Primary: Sign 1 paying client within 30 days
- Secondary: Book 10 sales calls, capture 50 qualified leads, publish 12 founder-led content pieces, and achieve one lead magnet funnel conversion rate above 3%

## Questions To Resolve

- Which vertical should be the first wedge: agencies, consultants, local service businesses, or B2B operators?
- What proof can be created fastest: teardown content, self-case-study, or pilot engagements?
- Which CTA will convert faster for a cold audience: paid audit, opportunity sprint, or free teardown call?
