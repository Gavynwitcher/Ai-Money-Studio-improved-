# Funnel And KPIs

## Funnel Map

| Stage | Asset | Copy angle | Conversion goal | Benchmark |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Awareness | LinkedIn posts, short-form videos, teardown content | "Your growth problem is structural, not motivational" | Drive profile visits and lead magnet traffic | Content should produce first meaningful lead within 30 days |
| Interest | Checklist lead magnet and teardown request CTA | "See the exact gaps in your offer, message, and funnel" | Convert traffic to email leads | 3 to 5 percent landing page conversion for cold traffic |
| Consideration | Email nurture, blog posts, and a simple case-study-style self-audit page | "Here is the process and why it works" | Move leads to booked calls | 35 to 45 percent open rate and 2 to 5 percent email CTR |
| Conversion | Discovery call plus Opportunity Sprint sales page | "Buy clarity and speed, not vague marketing labor" | Close first sprint | 10 to 20 percent booked-call-to-client rate early on |
| Retention | Weekly report, KPI review, experiment backlog, retainer upsell | "We keep improving what already works" | Convert build clients into monthly retainer | 30 percent upsell target from build clients |

## Offer Stack

- Core offer: Launch System Build at $4,000
- Upsell: Growth Optimization Retainer at $2,500 per month
- Cross-sell: Opportunity Sprint at $1,500 for colder leads not ready for a full build
- Guarantee: If the client finishes the intake and implementation tasks on time, they leave with a complete offer, message, and funnel action plan even if they do not continue
