# Market Signals

Date: 2026-04-05

## Why this agency angle is viable

- QuickBooks reported on June 25, 2025 that 68% of surveyed US small businesses use AI regularly and 74% say AI is boosting productivity.
- Intuit and ICIC's 2025 research said 89% of small businesses are leveraging AI, with more than 60% reporting increased productivity.
- HubSpot's 2025 AI reporting said 65% of marketing leaders planned to increase investment in AI and automation.
- Content Marketing Institute's 2025 B2B research found 45% of marketers lack a scalable content creation model and 47% struggle to measure results.
- Semrush's 2025 AI Overviews study found video carousels and discussion/forum visibility are becoming more important as AI changes search behavior.

## Benchmark assumptions used in this workspace

- Unbounce reported a median landing-page conversion rate of about 6.6% across industries based on Q4 2024 data, so this agency is using a more conservative 3 to 5 percent target for cold traffic.
- Unbounce's professional services benchmark noted paid social pages commonly convert at 3.4% or higher, which supports the early cold-traffic target.
- MailerLite reported a 2025 average open rate of 43.46%; this workspace uses a 35 to 45 percent open-rate target for warm nurture emails.
- Salesforce's email benchmark guidance says a general marketing email CTR in the 2 to 5 percent range is a reasonable benchmark, which is the target used here.
