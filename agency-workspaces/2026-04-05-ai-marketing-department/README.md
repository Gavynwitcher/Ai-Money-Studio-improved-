- Agency: AI Marketing Department

- Market: Marketing systems for founders and SMBs

## How To Use

1. Fill in `01-client-brief.md`.
2. Run the seven-phase workflow from `$ai-marketing-department`.
3. Store strategy outputs in the numbered files in order.
4. Use `06-weekly-report.md` for every active reporting cycle.
5. Keep campaign assets and exports in `campaigns/`, `deliverables/`, `reports/`, and `research/`.

## Working Rule

Do not produce launch assets until the opportunity, offer, and positioning files are credible.
